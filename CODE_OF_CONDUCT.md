# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Solar Fishing community aims to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive place for everyone who participates in project discussions, issue reports, pull requests, and other community spaces.

We welcome contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints. Participation in the project is expected to be professional and considerate.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate in discussions.
- Giving constructive feedback and accepting it gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than attacking people.
- Being patient with contributors who are learning.
- Respecting different experiences, abilities, and communication styles.
- Keeping discussions relevant and welcoming.
- Respecting other people's privacy and not sharing personal information without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Insults, deliberate humiliation, or repeated hostile comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or sexualized comments.
- Disrupting project discussions through sustained trolling or bad-faith behavior.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in Solar Fishing project spaces, including issue trackers, pull requests, community servers, and project-related discussions. It also applies when someone is representing the project or its community in other spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the project maintainers through an established project contact or moderation channel.

When reporting, include relevant context such as links, dates, and a description of what happened. Share only information that is necessary to explain the concern. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and deciding on an appropriate response. They may remove or edit harmful content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or remove someone from project spaces, depending on the circumstances.

Reports should be handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
