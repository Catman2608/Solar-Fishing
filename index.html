Overview

Questions about general settings, hotkeys, and basic options.

HOTKEYS DOESN'T WORK

Make sure to run as adminstrator on Windows and reset permissions after a Solar Fishing update on macOS

If that is checked, check the Start/Stop key, Area Selector key and Screenshot key in Overview match the keys you are pressing.

COULD NOT FIND THE ROBLOX GAME WINDOW

Open Roblox and join a game before you press Start. If Roblox is already open, please maximize the Roblox window. DO NOT RUN ROBLOX IN FULLSCREEN.

THE COLOR PICKER OR DETECTION LANDS IN THE WRONG PLACE

Press the Detect Roblox button and follow the prompt to open the area selector. This is caused by broken Roblox cache

IT BROKE AFTER I RESIZED OR MOVED ROBLOX

Stop the macro and start it again. This is because the macro gets the Roblox coordinates at the start.

MY GAME LAGS WHILE THE MACRO RUNS

The macro reads the screen many times a second. A low frame rate in Roblox makes the minigame harder to follow, so lower graphics directly helps tracking. Also, make sure to set controller mode to Predictive as it handles lag really well.

Cast

Issues related to casting, rod selection, and timing.

THE MACRO NEVER CASTS

Make sure your rod is in your hand. Set Rod hotbar slot key in General > Recovery so the macro can equip it itself. Also check nothing is open on screen, such as your inventory, a shop or a dialog box. The cast click lands on that instead.

MY CASTS ARE TOO SHORT OR TOO FAR

Normal cast: change Hold rod cast duration (ms) in Cast. Higher casts further out, lower casts closer in.

IT STARTS SHAKING BEFORE THE BOBBER LANDS

Raise Delay After Casting in Cast. Laggy servers need a bigger delay.

PERFECT CAST NEVER LETS GO

Go to the Cast tab and pick the green and white colors of the cast power bar again, or raise Green tolerance and White tolerance a little. If the bar is never found it gives up after Fall Scan Timeout and lets go anyway, so it will not hang forever.

PERFECT CAST LETS GO TOO EARLY OR TOO LATE

Change Release Delay and the velocity bands in Cast in steps of 0.1. Lower it if it lets go too late, raise it if it lets go too early.

PERFECT CAST LETS GO AT RANDOM MOMENTS

Lower Green tolerance and White tolerance. When they are too high, other white or green parts of the screen get mistaken for the cast bar.

Shake

Common problems with the shake detection and related settings.

IT CASTS BUT NEVER SHAKES

Check Shake mode in Shake. Pixel and Circle detect the Shake Area, Navigation uses the keyboard to press the shake button, which is more reliable.

SHAKING IS TOO SLOW AND THE FISH GETS AWAY

Lower Shake Delay in Shake. Going too low can make the game miss inputs, so lower it a little at a time.

IT HIT THE FAILSAFE BEFORE IT CAN STOP SHAKING

Increase Shake Failsafe (attempts) up by steps of 10. Repeat until it hits the minigame.

IT SHAKES FOREVER AND NEVER REACHES THE MINIGAME

Check the fish or friend area is actually being detected (see the Fish and Utilities tab). The macro keeps shaking until it sees the fish or friend area.

Fish

Minigame, catching, and fish-related troubleshooting.

ITS FISHING PERFECTLY BUT IT'S NOT CASTING

Go to the Fish tab, and increase restart delay by steps of 0.5.

Special Rods

Settings and issues specific to special or advanced rods.

ROD X WITHOUT SPECIAL MINIGAME DOESN'T WORK

The colors on the built-in configs might be wrong. Press the Pick Colors button and select the correct color, then send a bug report.

ROD X WITH SPECIAL MINIGAME DOESN'T WORK

Refer to the Tuning guide below.

Pinion's Aria tuning guide

Note Tracking Ratio is the ratio line on when the macro will start going to the note. Increase if it catches the note too early and lose the fish, decrease if it fail to catch the notes. (Increase or decrease by 0.1 each time and refer to the Note Ratio in the Status Overlay)

Tranquility Rod tuning guide

Tranquility Lead (ms) is the predictive timing offset (inspired by DeepFish ManiaLead). Increase if notes are hit too late, decrease if hit too early. Typical range 20-80. Velocity is auto-estimated from note movement.

Lullaby Rod tuning guide

Metronome Padding is the padding for the Lullaby to detect the hit area. Increase if detection fails, decrease if it spams the note and lose the fish. Mask Lost Ratio is the ratio for the mask to start hitting. (Increase or decrease by 0.1 each time and refer to the Mask Lost Ratio in the Status Overlay)

Noiseform tuning guide (Modern)

N/A

Noiseform tuning guide (Legacy)

White Threshold is the brightness level (0-255) used to detect the white Noiseform flash/zone. Black Threshold is the brightness level (0-255) used to detect the black Noiseform flash/zone. Higher values make white detection stricter; lower values make black detection stricter. Adjust by 10-20 while watching the status overlay.
